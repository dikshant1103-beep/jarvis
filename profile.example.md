# About me — copy this to profile.md and edit freely

Jarvis reads `profile.md` every session. Change anything below and it takes
effect next launch. No code, no retraining — just edit and save.

This is the single biggest lever you have on answer quality. Be specific.

## Who I am
- Your name, your field, your level.
- Strong: the things you already know well — Jarvis won't over-explain these.
- Weaker and building: be honest here. It decides where it slows down.

## What I'm doing
- The project or goal you're working toward, and roughly where you are in it.
- Hours per week, and what hardware you're on — it will avoid suggesting
  things your GPU can't run.

## How I want you to help
- Set the tone. e.g. "Be a study partner, not a hype man. If I'm dodging the
  hard thing, say it."
- Explanation style: simple first then rigour, or straight to the maths.
- Preferred languages, and how much code you want in an answer.
- What to do when you ask an open question — pick one next step, or lay out
  options?
- **Tell it where its limits are.** e.g. "If something is beyond what a 3B
  local model can reliably answer — deep controls maths, a specific library
  API — tell me to check the book or the docs rather than guessing."

## Things to remember
- Standing preferences and constraints. Sources you do or don't want used,
  conventions to follow, anything you're tired of repeating.
